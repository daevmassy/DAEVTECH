A good place to start

Practical answers, before you reach out.

Not sure which service fits? A short description of the issue or idea is enough to begin.

What should I include in my first message?

Tell us which service you are asking about, what you want to achieve and any device or setup details that seem relevant. A photo or screenshot can help explain a technical issue.

Do I need to know exactly what solution I need?

No. Describe the problem or result you have in mind and what you currently use. DAEV TECH can help clarify options and agree on a suitable scope.

How are cost and scope agreed?

They depend on the work involved. The requirements and proposed scope are discussed with you before work starts; ask for a quote when you get in touch.

Should I send my password to explain a support issue?

No. Do not share passwords or one-time sign-in codes by email or WhatsApp. Describe the issue instead; access arrangements can be discussed through an appropriate, secure process if needed.
